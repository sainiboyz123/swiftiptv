SwiftIPTV Support

For SwiftIPTV on iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Mac.

SwiftIPTV is a media player only. It does not provide, host, sell or include any channels, movies, series or playlists. You play only the playlists you add yourself, from a provider you are authorized to use.

Getting started

  1. Open SwiftIPTV and go to Settings.
  2. Add your playlist: paste an M3U/M3U8 playlist URL, or enter your Xtream Codes server, username and password from your provider.
  3. Wait for the sync to finish, then browse Live TV, Movies and Series.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I get a playlist?
From your own IPTV service provider. SwiftIPTV cannot supply one.

A channel shows a black screen or will not start.
Try the channel again, or change the player engine in Settings (Auto, Native or FFmpeg). Some streams are offline at the provider.

My playlist is slow to sync.
Large playlists can take a minute on the first sync; later updates are faster. Check that your provider's server is reachable.

How do I remove a playlist?
Settings > Playlists, then delete it. Its data is removed from your device.
