Privacy Policy

SwiftIPTV for iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and Mac · Effective October 13, 2026

Summary: SwiftIPTV does not collect, sell or share your personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What the app stores

The playlists you add (M3U URLs or Xtream Codes server details and login), your favorites, watch progress and settings are stored only on your device. Playlist logins are kept in the Apple Keychain. If you turn on iCloud sync, this data is stored in your own private iCloud account, which we cannot access.

Network connections

  • Your playlist provider: the app connects directly to the servers in the playlists you add, to download channel lists and play streams. Those servers are run by your provider, not by us, and their own privacy policies apply.
  • TMDB (optional): to show movie and series artwork and titles, the app may request public information from The Movie Database (TMDB) API. No personal information is sent; like any web request, TMDB receives your IP address. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Data we collect

None. We do not run servers that receive your data, and the app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.

Children

SwiftIPTV is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Remove a playlist in Settings, or delete the app, to erase its data from your device. Data synced to iCloud can be removed in your device's iCloud settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
